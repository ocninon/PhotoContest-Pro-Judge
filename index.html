Contest Mechanics

⚡ Quick Guide (TL;DR)

  • Start Time: Ensure your "Session Started" time is before the official deadline.
  • Rate All: Give scores (0-10) for Relevance, Creativity, & Impact.
  • Finish: Green checks = Done. Click "Finish Judging" to lock & rank.

Welcome to the PhotoContest Pro Judge panel. This tool allows you to evaluate and rank photos based on specific artistic criteria.

1. The Mechanics

  • Navigation: Scroll through the gallery to see all assigned photos.
  • Zooming: Click on any photo thumbnail to open the Inspection Mode. Hover your mouse over the image to activate the high-precision magnifier.
  • Progress Tracking: The top bar ("0 / 6 Scored") tracks your progress. A green checkmark ✓ will appear on photos you have fully judged.

2. Scoring Criteria

You must rate each photo from 0.0 to 10.0 on three distinct dimensions:

  • Relevance: How well does the photo fit the theme or subject matter?
  • Creativity: Is the composition, lighting, or angle unique and artistic?
  • Impact: Does the image capture attention and evoke emotion?

Tip: You can use the slider for rough scoring or type a precise decimal (e.g., 9.55) into the box.

3. Rating Scale Reference

Use the following standard guide to determine your scores:

9.0 - 10.0 Outstanding / Superior
Flawless technique, masterfully composed, profound impact.
7.0 - 8.9 Excellent / Very Good
Strong execution, minor flaws, distinct vision.
5.0 - 6.9 Good / Average
Competent but uninspired; technically adequate.
3.0 - 4.9 Weak / Below Average
Noticeable technical issues, lacks impact or composition.
0.0 - 2.9 Poor
Significant flaws, fails on multiple criteria.

4. Session Validation

The "Session Started" timestamp at the top of your screen is critical.

Why it matters: This timestamp proves when you began your judging session. It serves as validation that you commenced your evaluation on or before the official judging start time. A session started after the deadline may be disqualified.

5. Rules of Judging

  • Mandatory Scoring: You must provide a score for ALL three criteria for EVERY photo. The system will not let you finish if any score is missing.
  • No Skipped Photos: Ensure every card has a green checkmark before proceeding.

6. Finalizing Results

Once you are satisfied with your evaluations, click the "Finish Judging & Rank" button at the bottom.

⚠️ Important: Finalizing will LOCK ALL SCORES. You cannot go back and change ratings after this step. The system will then tabulate the results and declare the winners.
